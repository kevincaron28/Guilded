# Run your guild without the spreadsheet.

Guilded keeps EP/GP, loot rules, raid cores, signups and your guild's public page in one free
toolkit. Start in game with the addon alone; add the Discord bot when your guild wants it.

## Lite now, full Guilded after approval

Adding the bot puts your server on **Guilded Lite** right away and sends a request for full access.
You do not need to fill in a form.

| Works right away (Lite) | Unlocks after approval |
| --- | --- |
| `/setup start`: language, roles and channels | Addon and companion sync |
| `/help`, `/mod faq`, `/report bug` | EP/GP, loot, raid cores, seasons, attendance |
| `/dungeon group` and basic `/raid` signups | Dungeon leaderboards and your public guild page |
| | Weekly reports, reminders, polls and activities |

**How long does approval take?** Servers are approved by hand. When automatic approval is on, a
server is also approved by itself after a few days of normal use: `/setup start` done, a few
members, and someone used Guilded that week. Hosting space is limited (50 Lite servers at a time),
so approval is not guaranteed. Guilded is free: optional support never buys approval or priority.
Details: [hosted bot guide](/docs/HOSTED_PILOT.html).

## Set up your guild in five steps

1. **Add the bot** with the button above (you need permission to manage the server).
2. **Run `/setup start`** in Discord. Pick English or French, your time zone and the channels
   you want. `/setup start status:true` shows anything still missing. Move Guilded's role above
   the roles it must give.
3. **Ask members to install the addon** for your WoW edition: [download page](/editions/) and
   [member guide](/docs/MEMBER_INSTALL.html).
4. **After approval, connect the companion.** Members open the [online companion](/companion/),
   choose *Sign in with Discord*, then your server. At least one officer should run it: members
   who only use the addon reach Discord through an officer's sync.
5. **Go public when you are ready.** Create your guild page with `/guildpage create`, then
   `/guildpage publish`. Open recruitment with `/recruit set`. Publish your dungeon runs with
   `/mod leaderboard`. Share a one-night raid spot with `/raid pug share`.

Raid cores and GP prices: `/core setup`, `/core edit` and `/core items`. The [EP/GP guide](/docs/EPGP_GUIDE.html)
walks through a full raid night.

## Already using Raid-Helper?

Keep it. Both bots can share a server. Guilded's EP/GP, loot and standings come from the addon in
game, whatever your guild uses for signups.
