# Guilded — your guild, connected

Guilded links your **WoW Forever** guild to its Discord. Install the addon, sign in once with Discord, and rosters, dungeon scores, PvP, crafting and loot sync by themselves. The addon also works alone, with no Discord and no companion.

**For WoW Forever only. Addon in English, French, German and Spanish; companion, website and bot in English and French.**

## What's new in 9.0.4

- No more "blocked action" warning at login.
- The companion now syncs **several WoW accounts** on one PC by itself.
- Character pictures, record cards and item previews on the Discord and website side.

Guilded 9.0.x is Forever only: the other WoW versions were removed. Your saved data and SavedVariables are kept when you upgrade.

## Install and start

1. Download the latest Release file from this project's Files tab.
2. Close WoW and put the **Guilded** folder in `_forever_/Interface/AddOns`. Check that `Guilded/Guilded.toc` exists with no extra nested folder.
3. Log in and click the gold minimap coin, or type `/guilded`.
4. `/reload` saves your data. To sync with Discord, install the companion (below).

[Installation and troubleshooting / Installation en français](https://guilded.kcaron.top/docs/MEMBER_INSTALL)

## What you get

**Dungeons and seasons**
- Records every dungeon run with party, bosses and deaths; stats screens by dungeon, guild comparison and boss splits.
- Monthly seasons with weekly and monthly boards, level brackets, a traced death map, and guild records with a record card in Discord.

**PvP**
- Duels (scored by level difference), battlegrounds and world PvP stats with a guild ladder; a PvP group finder; call for help with location.

**Raids, EP/GP and loot**
- Raid sign-up in game and in Discord, attendance, EP/GP, loot history and loot rules (GP bidding, loot council, soft reserves).

**Guild life**
- Guild overview panel, in-game guild board with polls, roster tools for officers, guild bank view with requests.
- Crafting: guild crafters, recipes, cooldowns and in-game **crafting orders** linked to Discord.
- Guild chat tab (joins, online/offline), achievements, race to 60 and Hall of Fame.

**Discord side (bot and website)**
- Leaderboards and cards with **character pictures**, item previews and profession recipe cards, public ranking pages and guild pages.

## Optional Windows companion

The free [Microsoft Store companion](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N680XX3QFL9) is Store-signed and updates through the Store; a GitHub installer is also available. It sets itself up on first start (WoW folder, addon, Discord sign-in), syncs **every WoW account** on the PC and updates your in-game standings. Windows 10 (2004) or later, 64-bit.

[Get the Windows companion](https://guilded.kcaron.top/companion/)

## Optional Discord integration

Add the hosted bot to your server and run `/setup start`. A new server starts on **Guilded Lite** (setup, help, FAQ, bug reports, dungeon groups, basic raid sign-ups); KCaron's approval unlocks everything else. Independent hosting is also available. Each member signs in with Discord in the companion and picks their server.

Everything Guilded records about your characters is shared with your guild and on the public leaderboards by design; the one control is **Delete my data** on the website.
