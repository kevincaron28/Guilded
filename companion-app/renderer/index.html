How it works

  1. In game, your addon saves its data when you /reload or log out (/guilded sync does it for you).
  2. This app notices the change and sends it to the Discord bot within seconds.
  3. The bot answers with the newest standings, and this app writes them where the addon reads them.

Settings

Enter the bot address and server ID, link with /character pair, then choose your saved-data file.

Advanced

Danger zone

Stops the companion, turns off Start with Windows, and deletes the saved bot address, token and Discord link from this PC. Does not touch anything in World of Warcraft; set it up again any time by reopening Guilded Companion.
