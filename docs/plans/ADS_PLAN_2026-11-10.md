# Ads plan (website and Guilded Clash rewarded packs)

Status: **plan only, 10 November 2026.** Nothing here is built and no account has been opened. The owner picks; this document recommends.
Owner request (10 Nov): "plan ads on website and in mobile app; non-invasive ads for the website; in the mobile game, watch ads to open packs, with a maximum of 1-3 packs a day opened that way."
Builds on [MONETIZATION.md](MONETIZATION.md) (8 Nov: monetization is "later, not a priority"), [APP_TCG.md](APP_TCG.md) and [APP_TCG_GAME.md](APP_TCG_GAME.md).

Figures marked **(estimate)** are my assumptions from general industry ranges, not measurements and not read from a network's pricing page; check them when the account exists. Legal and tax points are **flags for a professional, not advice**.

## Summary and recommendation

1. **Ship Google Play closed testing WITHOUT ads, as drafted** (Ads = no, not a trader). Ads arrive in a later app update (9.2-era, after the 14-day closed test and production access), so the declarations never have to be reversed mid-review.
2. **Mobile first, because it is the better fit and the better money:** one rewarded video, opt-in only, to open **2 bonus packs a day** (recommended number), enforced on the server with AdMob server-side verification (SSV). Fallback when no ad loads: nothing is taken from anyone; the bonus simply is not offered (plus a free "come back later" line).
3. **Website: start with no network at all.** One static, self-hosted **sponsor slot** (one image or text card, `rel="sponsored"`, no script, no cookies) on public leaderboard and Hall of Fame pages only. Add one AdSense unit later only if a sponsor does not appear and the owner still wants it. Never on install, start, companion, sign-in, account or guild pages.
4. **Realistic money is small** (a few dollars to a few tens of dollars a month; see the tables). Ads pay out only past US$100 per network, so the first payout may be many months away. The reason to do it is the owner's wish and learning, not income; the plan keeps cost and risk equally small.
5. **The owner-side blockers are not code:** the "not a trader" Play status must change (public contact details), an AdMob account, a payments profile, and a short professional read on Quebec/Canadian registration and tax.

## 1. Website (guilded.kcaron.top)

### What the site is today (read from the repo)

- Static pages built from `site/pages/*.md` (start, companion, features, officers, guides, reference, Quebec Gold) and served by Cloudflare; the Worker (`worker/index.mjs`) proxies everything dynamic to the bot.
- **Leaderboards, recruitment, profiles and guild pages are rendered by the bot** (`src/services/web-layout.ts`: server-rendered, "no scripts: the pages work under a strict Content-Security-Policy"). They already never load analytics (`docs/PRIVACY.md`).
- Static pages ship a strict CSP in `site/_headers`: `script-src 'self'`, `connect-src 'self'`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`. **Any third-party ad script requires loosening that CSP** (script, frame, img and connect origins) for the pages that carry it, which is a real cost in security posture, not just wording.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics is wired but off (`site/analytics.json` token is empty). So **there is no traffic data**. Step zero is to switch it on (cookie-free; see below) and measure two weeks before any ad decision.
- Audience: Québec Gold has about 240 members and three hosted servers; public boards also draw search and Discord-link visitors. The site is bilingual (EN/FR), so any ad copy and the privacy text need both.

### Ad options for a small gaming-community site

| Option | Fit | Needs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Direct sponsor/affiliate slot** (static card; for example a WoW-related creator, a gaming peripheral store affiliate link, a guild-friendly service) | **Best fit.** No script, no cookies, no consent banner for the slot itself, no CSP change, no layout shift | Owner finds and invoices a sponsor, or an affiliate account (store referral links) | Revenue is whatever is agreed; probably zero for a long while. Must carry a visible "Sponsored" label (and `rel="sponsored noopener"`). Affiliate links need disclosure too |
| **Google AdSense** (one responsive unit) | Works at any traffic; approval needs original content and a privacy policy; fills with whatever Google sells | AdSense account, `ads.txt`, CSP loosening, **consent management platform (CMP)** | Heaviest page cost; can show off-theme or autoplay-video-like creatives unless categories are blocked. Pays out at US$100 |
| **Gaming network** (Venatus, Playwire, Mediavine, Raptive, Ezoic) | Poor fit. Typical minimums are tens of thousands of monthly sessions or pageviews (check each; thresholds change) | Traffic we do not have | Revisit only if the public boards ever grow far past the guild |
| **EthicalAds / Carbon** (single privacy-friendly unit, no tracking) | Attractive on paper (one unit, no cookies), but both target developer, design and tech audiences and approve publishers by audience | An application; likely a decline for a WoW guild site | Worth one application because rejection is free; not a plan |
| **Ko-fi / "support the server" link** | Already exists (MONETIZATION option A) | none | Not an ad, but the most honest non-invasive "monetization" for this audience; keep it |

**Recommendation:** direct sponsor slot first (it can be live in under a day and needs nothing from visitors); AdSense is the fallback, and only after two weeks of measured traffic show it is worth a consent banner.

### Where a non-invasive unit could go

One unit per page, in the page body below the main content or beside it on wide screens, never above the fold on phone.

| Place | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Public leaderboards (dungeons, characters, guilds, world PvP), Hall of Fame, season pages (bot-rendered) | **Yes, candidates.** Public, read-for-curiosity pages, visited by non-members |
| Public guides that are not part of setup (for example a future dungeon guide or the reference page) | Yes, below the content |
| `/start/`, `/companion/`, guide-install, guide-link, downloads, status | **Never.** First-run pages, where trust matters and a misclick could mislead a user toward the wrong download |
| Sign-in, `/me/*`, account, officer and guild pages members use daily | **Never** |
| The addon, the Windows companion and the phone app screens other than the reward flow | **Never** (Blizzard policy covers the addon; see section 3) |

Rules for every unit: no popups, no interstitials, no sticky bars, no autoplay or sound, no expanding creatives, no ad above the page title, a reserved box height so nothing shifts, a visible "Sponsored" label, an officer/owner off switch per server for guild public pages, and block categories (gambling, alcohol, adult, "get rich", gold-selling and boosting/RMT ads, which would also contradict Blizzard's rules) in AdSense if it is ever used.

### Revenue at realistic traffic (estimate)

No analytics exist yet, so I model three levels. Display ads on small gaming sites commonly earn roughly US$1-5 per 1,000 pageviews (RPM); a direct sponsor is a flat fee we cannot predict.

| Monthly pageviews on ad pages | At RPM US$1 | At US$3 | At US$5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 (guild-sized) | US$1 | US$3 | US$5 |
| 5,000 | US$5 | US$15 | US$25 |
| 20,000 (a good month for a niche board) | US$20 | US$60 | US$100 |

So **at guild scale it is single dollars per month**; with a US$100 payout threshold, an AdSense account would take a year or more to pay once. A sponsor slot at even C$10-25 a month beats it. This is why the recommendation is "sponsor slot, no consent banner" rather than a network.

### Consent banner (EU, UK, Quebec Law 25)

- **No ads = no banner.** A static sponsor slot with no third-party request, no cookie and no identifier needs no consent banner. This is the main reason to prefer it.
- **AdSense or any network** sets cookies and identifiers for ad selection and measurement. For EEA and UK visitors Google requires a **Google-certified CMP** (Google's own free Funding Choices works; the IAB TCF string is needed). For Quebec, Law 25 requires express opt-in consent before technology that profiles or identifies a person ("tracking"), a clear explanation in French, and a privacy officer and contact point; the rest of Canada follows PIPEDA (meaningful consent). In practice: **show the CMP to everyone, defaults off, "Decline" as prominent as "Accept", French and English, and run non-personalized or contextual-only ads for anyone who declines.** Confirm with a professional how Law 25 applies to a free hobby site that earns money.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookie-free, no fingerprinting) is already described in the privacy policy and needs no banner; keep it separate from ads.

### Privacy policy changes (website and bot pages)

`docs/PRIVACY.md` (EN and FR) currently says the bot pages "never load" analytics and says nothing about advertising. When any ad goes live, add: who the advertiser or network is; what is collected (for a static sponsor slot: nothing, plus a count of clicks if the link redirects through `/go/<id>`); the consent choice and how to change it; cookie list and lifetimes; that pages used for sign-in and setup carry no ads; and the retention and deletion path (Delete my data is unchanged). Also Law 25 contact details of the person responsible for personal information. Update the same text in the Play listing and Data safety (section 3).

### Performance impact

- Sponsor slot: one image, preloaded size reserved, `loading="lazy"`; effectively zero.
- AdSense: typically adds several requests and several hundred kilobytes of script and creatives, affects Largest Contentful Paint and layout shift unless the box is reserved, and needs the CSP relaxed. Keep it out of the static `_headers` defaults; add it to a path-specific `_headers` block only for ad pages. Bot-rendered pages need their own CSP change in `web-layout.ts`, which is why the sponsor slot (image from our own origin, no script) is far less invasive.

## 2. Mobile app: rewarded video to open a card pack

### What exists (read from the repo)

- Packs: server-authoritative. `rollPack` in `src/services/tcg.ts` rolls a 5-card pack (four Common/Uncommon slots, one Rare-or-better slot, Epic pity at 10 packs, Legendary pity at 40, duplicate protection), and `openPack` in `src/services/tcg-store.ts` spends a banked pack with a compare-and-set on `TcgAccount`, logging to `TcgLog`. The phone calls `POST /api/v1/companion/cards/open` and never supplies randomness.
- Free supply today: one pack accrues every 20 hours (cap 3 banked); play can give up to `MAX_PLAY_PACKS_PER_DAY = 3` packs from quests and wins (`src/services/tcg-quests.ts`, daily counters in `TcgDaily`, day key = UTC date). A pack the bank cannot hold converts to 60 dust.
- Ranked play uses the same collection and decks; ranked rewards are cosmetic (APP_TCG_GAME.md 4.2). Guild trading exists (`tcg-trade.ts`).
- Mobile has no ads or analytics SDK (`mobile/package.json`; `mobile/store/play-data-safety.md` states so). `app.json` lists only `expo-secure-store` and `expo-notifications`.

### Which SDK

| Option | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| **Google AdMob through `react-native-google-mobile-ads` (Invertase)** with its Expo config plugin | **Recommended.** The maintained RN wrapper for AdMob, with a built-in `AdsConsent` (Google UMP) API, rewarded ads, and server-side verification options (`setServerSideVerificationOptions({ userId, customData })`). Needs an Expo **development build** (EAS), not Expo Go; the app already builds with EAS. Confirm it supports Expo SDK 58 / React Native 0.88 before committing (compatibility lag is the usual risk) |
| AppLovin MAX or Unity LevelPlay (mediation) | More demand sources and higher eCPM, but heavier SDKs, more declarations, more privacy surface. Overkill at this scale; revisit only if AdMob fill is poor |
| Meta Audience Network, IronSource directly | No benefit over AdMob for a small app; more SDKs to declare |
| Expo's own ad module | Does not exist as a first-party module; do not wait for one |

Use AdMob test ad units in all development builds, real units only in the production build, and never click your own real ads.

### The design: the server owns the cap, the phone only plays the video

Why the server: the phone is the least trusted part. A client counter can be edited, replayed or skipped, and a "reward earned" call from the phone proves nothing. AdMob's **server-side verification (SSV)** has Google call our server when a user earns a reward, signed with a key Google publishes.

Flow:

1. **Ask.** The phone calls `POST /api/v1/companion/cards/ad/start`. The server checks the account (collection on, cap not reached, cooldown) and creates an `AdGrant` row `{ id, discordUserId, day, status: "pending", expiresAt: now + 10 min }`. It returns the grant `id` (an opaque random string) and how many are left today. The server answers "none left" before any ad loads, so players are never shown an ad that cannot pay.
2. **Show.** The phone loads a rewarded ad and sets `setServerSideVerificationOptions({ userId: <opaque account id>, customData: <grant id> })`. The user id sent to Google is a hashed account id, never the Discord id, name or e-mail.
3. **Google calls us.** When the viewer earns the reward, Google sends a GET to our callback, for example `https://guilded.kcaron.top/api/v1/ads/ssv?...` with `ad_network, ad_unit, custom_data, reward_amount, reward_item, timestamp, transaction_id, user_id, signature, key_id`. The server verifies the ECDSA signature over the query string against Google's published verifier keys (cache them, refresh on an unknown `key_id`), looks up the grant by `custom_data`, checks it is `pending`, unexpired and owned by that `user_id`, and **checks `transaction_id` against a unique constraint so a replay is rejected**. Then it marks the grant `earned` and increments that account's ad counter for the day inside one transaction. No ad-pack is credited by the phone.
4. **Claim.** The phone polls `POST /api/v1/companion/cards/ad/claim` with the grant id (SSV may arrive a few seconds late; poll up to about 30 seconds). If the grant is `earned`, the server rolls the bonus pack through the same `rollPack` and `TcgLog` path (kind `"adpack"`), marks the grant `claimed`, and returns the pack exactly like `cards/open`. A `claimed` grant cannot be claimed again.
5. **Failure modes.** Ad closed early: grant expires, nothing counted. SSV never arrives (network): the grant stays pending and expires; the player is not charged a daily slot (the counter increments on `earned`, not `start`). The Worker must allow-list the new routes and the callback path (`worker/index.mjs` allow-list; the callback uses a query string and needs no extra headers).

New data (one hand-written migration, per repo rules; no existing table changes): `TcgAdGrant { id, discordUserId, day, status, transactionId? @unique, createdAt, earnedAt?, claimedAt?, expiresAt }` and a counter either on `TcgDaily` (new `adPacks Int @default(0)`) or derived by counting `claimed` grants for the day. Counting grants is simpler and cannot drift.

**Daily cap and reset:** a hard server constant `AD_PACKS_PER_DAY` (recommended 2; the code accepts 1-3) checked at `start` and again at claim, in the same transaction as the grant update. The day uses the same `dayKey` (UTC date) as the quests so there is one reset to explain; that reset is in the evening in Quebec (about 19:00-20:00), which is why an owner decision below offers a 05:00 UTC reset for both. Add a per-grant cooldown (for example 60 seconds between ads) and a global kill switch `ADS_ENABLED` and a per-guild off switch (repo rule 5: every feature has officer and member off switches; the officer switch hides the offer entirely).

**Anti-abuse:**

- SSV signature and `transaction_id` uniqueness stop forged and replayed rewards; a rooted phone or emulator cannot mint packs because the pack is rolled server-side after Google's callback.
- Per-account cap plus per-device and per-IP soft limits on `start` (reuse `allowCompanionRevocation`-style rate limits seen in `companion-api.ts`); the cap is on the Discord account, which is tied to a linked guild member, so a new alt costs a real guild membership.
- Trading is the amplifier: ads on many alts, cards funnelled to one main. Existing trade caps and the alt-farming rule in APP_TCG_GAME.md section 6 apply; additionally **mark cards from ad packs untradeable for 24 hours** or count ad packs in the daily "net value received" flag. Recommended: the flag only (simplest), review after a month.
- Log every grant (`status`, timestamps, user, transaction) for the AdMob "invalid traffic" conversation if an account is ever flagged. Never offer a reward for clicking an ad; the reward is for watching only (AdMob policy forbids incentivizing clicks).
- Do not reward a user's own test ads in production: SSV callbacks for test units come with a test marker; ignore them.

### How it fits the pack economy and ranked play (never pay-to-win)

- Ads are **time-for-progress, never power for money**: no purchase exists in the app (APP_TCG.md hard rule: no real-money purchases), and any member can watch the same capped number. Ranked rewards stay cosmetic.
- Current free supply is about 1.2 packs a day banked plus up to 3 from play. The design target is about 183 packs for a median full collection, "two to three months at 2 to 3 packs a day" (APP_TCG_GAME.md simulation). **Two ad packs a day would raise the ceiling of an engaged player from about 3 to about 5 packs a day (roughly 40-65% faster)**, so run the simulation again before launch and move dust and craft prices only if the finish time drops below the target. Three would shorten completion to well under two months, which cuts the long tail that makes ranked meaningful, so I recommend **2**.
- **Content of an ad pack: a normal 5-card pack** through the same `rollPack` with shared pity counters, so a watcher is never in a worse economy than a non-watcher, and the odds screen needs no second table. (A smaller 3-card "bonus pack" is the alternative if the simulation shows too fast; it adds a second odds table and a "why is this smaller" explanation, so I would not start there.)
- **Ad packs do not accrue in the bank and cannot be stockpiled.** They are rolled and shown at claim time. If an officer has switched the collection off for the player's server in the meantime, nothing is granted and the grant is voided.
- Ad packs count toward `packsOpened` and pity; they do not count toward the 3-a-day play cap, so ads never take the place of earning by playing.
- No ads inside matches, in ranked, in the lobby, or in any game state: the only ad surface is a clearly labelled button on the pack screen.

The pack screen shows, when ad packs are available: **"Watch a short video for a bonus pack (1 of 2 today)"**. Rules: opt-in tap only, always skippable (close the player before the end: nothing is granted, nothing is lost), never auto-played, no countdown pressure, no "you will lose" language.

## 3. Store and legal consequences

### Google Play

- **"Contains ads" declaration:** currently "no ads" in the Play Console draft (`android-play-console-9-nov.md`). It must become **Yes** in the same release that ships the SDK. Do not ship the SDK under a "no ads" declaration (policy violation, can suspend the app).
- **Data safety:** `mobile/store/play-data-safety.md` says no ads SDK, no advertising ID. With AdMob, add **Device or other IDs (advertising ID)** and approximate **location (IP-derived)**, **App interactions** and **Diagnostics** as collected, shared with a third party (Google) for **Advertising or marketing** and **Analytics**; shared data cannot be "optional/not shared". Declare the Android **`AD_ID`** permission in the manifest (the config plugin adds it when needed) and fill the Advertising ID declaration (currently "No"). Update `play-data-safety.md` and the privacy URL at the same time.
- **Target age and Families:** target is 13-15, 16-17, 18+ and not child-appealing (`mobile/store/play-content-rating.md`), which keeps the app out of the Families programme. That matters: if the target ever includes under 13, only Families-certified ad SDKs and formats are allowed. Keep Discord's 13+ rule. In AdMob set the maximum ad content rating (use "T", Teen) and **tag users under the age of consent (TFUA)** when we know or suspect age; we do not know ages, so request non-personalized ads for players under 16/18 where the account's age is known, and otherwise follow the consent result.
- **User consent (UMP):** use Google's User Messaging Platform through `AdsConsent` before loading ads: required for EEA, UK and Switzerland; also use it for Quebec/Canada visitors as a conservative default (Law 25). Offer the "Ad choices" re-open.
- **Trader status (the owner is declared "not a trader").** Google's EU Digital Services Act rule: a developer acting for business, trade or professional purposes must be declared a **trader** and show a verified **name, address, phone number and e-mail publicly** on the store listing (the owner is in Canada, but the listing shows in the EU). Free hobby apps can stay "not a trader"; an app that earns revenue (ads, in-app items, subscriptions) is generally treated as commercial. **Moving to ads therefore means switching the declaration and publishing contact details**, probably through a business address or mailbox the owner is willing to show. Verify the wording on the Play Console's own trader-status page when the time comes and **ask a professional whether ad income makes the owner a business** (see below). If the owner prefers to stay a non-trader, there are no ads.
- **Payments profile:** AdMob payouts need a Google payments profile with a verified address and tax information (W-8BEN-style forms for non-US publishers; the Console asks). Payout threshold US$100.
- **Rewarded ads policy:** the reward must be disclosed before the ad, the ad must be skippable by the user closing it without penalty beyond not receiving the reward, no incentives to click, and rewarded placements must not be forced.
- **Store text:** `mobile/store/play-listing.md` currently says (EN/FR) things like "no packs, no chance, nothing to buy" for the badge album; Guilded Clash has packs. The listing and its "no ads" line must match the shipped build each time. When ads arrive, add a line such as "Optional rewarded video for bonus packs. Nothing to buy."
- Loot-box rules: packs are free and random, with the odds screen already specified (APP_TCG.md). Nothing is purchasable, so Google/Apple loot-box purchase disclosures do not apply; a rewarded ad is not a payment. Keep it that way; review with a professional if a paid pack is ever considered (the plan says never).

### Apple (later)

An iOS ads build needs the Apple Developer Program (not yet bought), an **App Tracking Transparency** prompt before any IDFA use (or run non-personalized and skip ATT), **App Privacy** labels updated for ads, the AdMob iOS app id in `app.json`, and a review note explaining the rewarded placement. Apple has its own trader status in the EU. Do this only after Android ads are stable (track M5).

### Blizzard and the WoW addon

- **No ads in the addon, ever** (Blizzard's UI add-on policy, summarized in MONETIZATION.md constraint 1). No ad, sponsor link or "watch to unlock" appears in Lua, in tooltips, chat output, or the companion's in-game output.
- A website or phone app that carries ads around WoW content is outside the addon but inside Blizzard's **fan content rules**. From memory of Blizzard's Community Content/"Fan Content Policy" (verify the current text before launch): fan sites may use Blizzard names and imagery only for non-commercial fan purposes, so a site that **monetizes with ads next to Blizzard assets** is the risk. Mitigation: keep the sponsor slot away from pages that reproduce Blizzard art, class icons or game logos (our leaderboards show class and name text; check that we use no ripped game art), do not use Blizzard marks in ad copy, keep the "not affiliated with Blizzard" line, and never run gold-selling, boosting or RMT ads. Guilded Clash already uses original art and generic faction names (APP_TCG_GAME.md), which keeps the game itself clear.
- The PolyForm Noncommercial licence on the code (MONETIZATION.md constraint 2) is the owner's to license; adding ads to the owner's own hosted site does not bind them, but a one-paragraph statement and a short legal read are recommended before any income.

### Canadian / Quebec registration and tax (flag for a professional)

Not advice. Topics to raise with an accountant or lawyer before the first payout:

- Whether ad and sponsorship income makes the activity a **business** (sole proprietorship) rather than a hobby; **business registration in Quebec** (the Registraire des entreprises' rules for a sole proprietor using a business name) and whether the public trader address needs a business address.
