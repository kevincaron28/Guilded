<h1 id="link-title">Link your <em>character.</em></h1>
<p>Linking tells Guilded which WoW characters are yours, so your guild's Discord and the website show them. With the companion it is one sign-in and one <code>/reload</code>: nothing to type or copy.</p>
<p class="small">Not installed yet? Start with <a href="/guides/install/">Install Guilded</a>. You also need to be in your guild's Discord server.</p>
</section>

<section class="start-path" aria-labelledby="companion-link-title">
<h2 id="companion-link-title">With the Windows companion (easiest)</h2>
<ol class="start-list">
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><h3>Press Sign in with Discord</h3></div><p>Open the companion and choose <strong>Link everything</strong> in the left menu, then press <strong>Sign in with Discord</strong>.</p><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-companion-link-everything.png" alt="The companion Link everything page with the Sign in with Discord button" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>Link everything, Sign in with Discord highlighted.</figcaption></figure></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><h3>Choose your server and press Connect</h3></div><p>Your browser opens (it may hide behind the companion window: press <strong>Reopen the page</strong> if you do not see it). Sign in to Discord if asked, choose your guild's server, then press <strong>Connect</strong>. Come back to the companion. If the page asks for a code, type the one shown in the companion.</p><p class="small">Discord may email you to confirm a new login location: open the email, choose <strong>Yes, it's me</strong>, then continue.</p><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-browser-connect.png" alt="The Connect your companion web page with the server choice and the Connect button" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>the browser page with the server choice and Connect.</figcaption></figure></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><h3>Type /reload in game</h3></div><p>In WoW, log in on your character and type <code>/reload</code> once. WoW saves your data and the companion reads it. If the companion has not noticed yet, press <strong>Detect now</strong> (or <strong>Find my saved data</strong>) and type <code>/reload</code> again.</p><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-ingame-reload.png" alt="The WoW chat box with the /reload command typed" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>WoW chat with /reload typed.</figcaption></figure></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><h3>See that it is linked</h3></div><p>You are linked when the companion shows a green <strong>✓</strong>, <em>Everything is linked. Nothing more to do.</em>, and your characters in the <strong>Your characters</strong> table with <strong>✓ Linked</strong>. Guilded also sends you a short Discord message naming the characters it linked. Your main and every alt that logged in on this PC link at the same time.</p><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-companion-linked.png" alt="The companion Link everything page with all links done and the characters table showing Linked" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>companion with the green check, Everything is linked and the characters table.</figcaption></figure><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-discord-confirmation.png" alt="The Discord message confirming which characters were linked" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>the Discord message that says which characters were linked.</figcaption></figure><p>In Discord, <code>/character list</code> shows your main and alts at any time.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
<section class="start-path" id="no-companion" aria-labelledby="verify-title">
<h2 id="verify-title">Without the companion: one line from Discord</h2>
<p>No Windows PC, or you would rather not install the companion? Use this instead. At least one guildmate (usually an officer) needs to run the companion, because their sync is what completes your link.</p>
<ol class="start-list">
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><h3>Ask Discord for your line</h3></div><p>In your guild's Discord, type <code>/character pair</code>. Guilded answers privately. Copy the line that looks like <code>/guilded verify</code> followed by your code.</p><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-discord-character-pair.png" alt="The Discord reply to /character pair showing the /guilded verify line to copy" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>the /character pair reply with the verify line highlighted.</figcaption></figure></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><h3>Paste it in WoW and press Enter</h3></div><p>In game, paste the whole line into the chat box of any of your characters and press <strong>Enter</strong>. The game answers that your Discord link code is saved and that your characters link by themselves.</p><figure class="guide-shot"><img class="guide-shot-img" src="/assets/guides/link-ingame-verify.png" alt="The WoW chat box after /guilded verify, with the saved confirmation message" loading="lazy"><figcaption><span class="shot-soon">Screenshot coming soon: </span>WoW chat after /guilded verify with the confirmation line.</figcaption></figure></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><h3>Wait for the Discord message</h3></div><p>The link completes the next time a guildmate syncs with the companion (often within the hour). Guilded then sends you a Discord message saying which character is linked. Once is enough, also for your alts.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
<section class="start-path" aria-labelledby="second-title">
<h2 id="second-title">Linking a second character</h2>
<p>Most of the time you do nothing.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Same PC, with the companion:</strong> log in on the alt, type <code>/reload</code>, then press <strong>Sync now</strong> in the companion. It joins the others.</li>
<li><strong>Another PC:</strong> install the companion there too and press <strong>Sign in with Discord</strong> again. Your other PC stays linked.</li>
<li><strong>With the verify line:</strong> your code already covers every character on your WoW account.</li>
<li><strong>A character is missing?</strong> In Discord type <code>/character claim</code> and pick it from the list. Check with <code>/character list</code>.</li>
<li><strong>A character is wrong?</strong> In Discord type <code>/character unlink</code> and pick it.</li>
</ul>
<h3>It did not work?</h3>
<div class="start-help"><details><summary>The browser did not open, or I lost the page</summary><p>In the companion press <strong>Reopen the page</strong>. The window may be behind the companion or in the taskbar.</p></details><details><summary>No character appears after /reload</summary><p>Make sure Guilded is ticked in the game's AddOns list, type <code>/reload</code>, then press <strong>Check my characters</strong> after a minute. Still empty: in Discord, <code>/character claim</code>.</p></details><details><summary>The companion says I am not in a server that uses Guilded</summary><p>Join your guild's Discord (ask an officer for the invite), then press <strong>Link to my guild</strong> in the companion.</p></details><details><summary>I want to undo it</summary><p>In the companion press <strong>Manage connected devices</strong> to remove this PC, or <strong>Unlink this PC</strong>. In Discord, <code>/character unlink</code> removes one character.</p></details></div>
