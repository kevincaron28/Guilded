WHAT IT DOES

Everything your guild tracks, in one addon.

EP/GP and loot

Priorities, awards and history, under rules your officers choose.

Crafters and readiness

See who can craft what, who is ready, and how fresh the data is.

Dungeon leaderboards

Guilded Dungeons records runs and ranks them for each WoW version.

Optional Discord

Standings and sign-ups in Discord when your guild wants them. Never required.

GUILDED GUIDES · EN / FR

The right folder, a first reload, and fixes if something is missing.

Points, priorities, core pools and a complete raid-to-standings check.
