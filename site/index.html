QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Stuck on setup?

Open the help guide ↗
Is it free?

Yes, Guilded is free for noncommercial use. Tips help pay for hosting, but you never need to pay to download it, get the bot or ask for help.

Do I need Discord?

No. The addon works on its own. Discord and the companion only add optional sync.

How do I get the bot for my guild?

The hosted bot is a small free pilot: KCaron approves each Discord server before setup works. Follow the pilot guide or host your own bot.

My guild hosts its own bot. Where is my companion?

Ask your officer for the HTTPS address. Never put a token or pairing code here.
