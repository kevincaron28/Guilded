# Guilded 9.5.0 release notes

Published 22 November 2026. Protocol 2, WoW Forever only. Bot changes are already live.

Real-client checks: docs/V9_5_0_RELEASE_ACCEPTANCE.md.

## Addon

- Guild events feed: joins, leaves and rank changes show in your chat, and `/guilded events` lists the recent ones. Turn it off in Settings (#1253).
- Your saved file is smaller (#1248).
- More lines in French. Layer auto-invite stays off unless you turn it on (#1258).

## Companion

- Version 9.5.0, no other change.

## Phone app 0.5.1

- Sign up for a raid from your phone (I'm in, Maybe, Can't come) and get reminders 24 h, 2 h and 1 h before (#1259).
- The News tab shows WoW news again (#1249, #1252, #1256).

## Bot (already live)

- French for members uses "tu"; slash commands and error messages are in French on French servers (#1254, #1257).
- Discord Linked Roles: show your verified WoW character on your Discord profile; servers can require it for a role (#1255).
- Thank-you cards for guild bank donors, including everyone who gave before (#1242, #1244).
- Card and wording fixes from a full review of the bot's posts (#1245, #1246, #1247, #1250, #1251).

## Français (résumé)

- Addon : fil des événements de guilde (arrivées, départs, rangs) et `/guilded events`; fichier de sauvegarde plus petit; plus de lignes en français.
- Téléphone 0.5.1 : inscription aux raids et rappels 24 h, 2 h et 1 h avant; l'onglet Nouvelles fonctionne.
- Bot : français en « tu », commandes en français, rôles liés Discord, cartes de remerciement pour les dons à la banque.
