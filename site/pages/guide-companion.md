# The Windows companion

The companion is optional: the addon works alone, and your dungeon runs and duels can reach the
leaderboards through another player’s companion. It is the fastest way to link your characters to
Discord (without it, send the Guilded bot your character’s full name in a direct message), and it
syncs by itself. It is free, for Windows 10 and 11.

## Install it

1. [Download it](/download/#companion), or get it from the Microsoft Store, where it is listed as
   **Guild Sync Companion**.
2. Run the file. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info → Run anyway** (the website
   installer is not code-signed).
3. Leave it running. It starts with Windows and sits near the clock. It finds World of Warcraft,
   installs or updates the Guilded addon, and picks your guild’s Discord server from your in-game guild.
4. In WoW, type `/reload` (or log out). The **Last sync** line turns green with the time.

## What the window shows

| Line or button | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **World of Warcraft** | The game folder it uses. Not found? Press **Choose folder** and pick the World of Warcraft folder that holds `_forever_`. |
| **Guilded addon** | The addon is installed and up to date. |
| **Last sync** | When your data was last sent. **Sync now** sends it at once. |
| **Synced as** | Your Discord name once you sign in. **Sign in with Discord** is optional: it confirms your characters and unlocks Discord messages. |
| **Advanced** | **Repair**, **Reinstall the addon**, **WoW accounts**, **All problems**, **Activity** and **Copy support report**. |

## Updates

The companion updates itself: a new version installs when you quit or restart it, or at once with
**Update**. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.

## Several WoW accounts on one PC

When it finds Guilded’s data on another account, it asks whether that account is yours: **Yes** syncs
it too, **No** leaves it alone (for someone who shares your PC). Change the answer later under
**Advanced → WoW accounts**.
