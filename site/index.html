    QUESTIONS

    Good to know.

    Setup help →
    Ask on Discord ↗

    Is it free?

    Yes, for noncommercial use. Tips help pay for hosting but are never required. Buy me a coffee ↗

    Do I need Discord or the companion?

    No. The addon shares everything with your guild in game. Discord and the companion are only for taking it online.

    How do I get the bot for my guild?

    Add it to your Discord server. The basics (Guilded Lite) work right away; addon sync, EP/GP and seasons unlock once your server is approved. Guide for officers · Host your own

    We already use Raid-Helper. Can we keep it?

    Yes. Both bots can share a server. Guilded’s EP/GP, loot and standings come from the addon in game, whatever you use for signups.

    My guild hosts its own bot. Where is my companion?

    Ask your officer for the HTTPS address. Never put a token or pairing code here.
