# Google Play content rating (IARC questionnaire) — Guilded

French copy: [play-content-rating.fr.md](play-content-rating.fr.md). Guilded Clash is not in the published app, so nothing here covers it.

Where to fill it in: Play Console → **Policy → App content → Content ratings → Start questionnaire**. Use the
support contact email from [play-listing.md](play-listing.md), not a personal address.

## Category

**All Other App Types** (utility, productivity, communication or other). Why not the other categories:

- Not a game: it is a companion for a game.
- Not "Social networking, forums, blogs and UGC sharing": members cannot post messages, chat, comment or upload media.
  What other members see is game data: characters, ranks, last-seen positions inside the game, wishlist item names.
- Not "Reference, news or educational": the News tab is one of five tabs and only shows headlines that link out.

## Answers (submitted 15 November 2026)

The questionnaire Play showed on 15 November 2026 for **All Other App Types**, with Play's own wording, and the answers
the owner gave. Contact email: `support@guildedwow.com`. Completing it accepts the IARC terms of use.

| # | Play's question | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Does the app contain any ratings-relevant content (e.g., sex, violence, language) downloaded as part of the app package (code, assets)? | No | Text and numbers only; no game footage or art. |
| 2 | Does the app natively allow users to interact or exchange content with other users through voice communication, text, or sharing images or audio? | **No** (owner, 15 November 2026) | There is no chat, voice, picture or audio sharing. Guildmates see each other's characters, raid sign-ups, wishlist item names and last-seen positions inside the game, which is guild data, not a conversation. Answering Yes opens seven questions about public sharing, blocking, reporting and chat moderation that do not describe this app. |
| 3 | Does the app feature or promote content that isn't part of the initial app download, but can be accessed from the app? (Play's examples: movies in Netflix, product listings in Amazon Shopping, songs in Spotify) | **No** (owner, 15 November 2026) | The app shows the guild's own data and public leaderboards, and News lists headlines that open in the phone's browser. It is not a catalogue of films, music or products. |
| 4 | Does the app focus on promoting or selling items or activities that are typically age-restricted such as cigarettes, alcohol, firearms, or gambling? | No | |
| 5 | Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with other users? | No | The app never reads the device location. The guild map shows positions inside the game, sent by the PC companion. |
| 6 | Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? | No | No purchases. |
| 7 | Does the app include cash rewards, gift cards, play-to-earn features, convertible cryptocurrency rewards, or the issuance of transferable digital assets (e.g., NFTs)? | No | |
| 8 | Is the app a web browser or search engine? | No | |
| 9 | Is the app primarily a news or educational product? | No | News is one tab of five. |

**Ratings Play returned:** ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, USK all ages, ClassInd (Brazil) all ages, IARC Generic 3+, Google Play
3+. There is no "Users Interact" element, because question 2 is No.

The questionnaire it replaced had been filled in as a **Game** with digital purchases, random prizes and moderated chat
(from the card-game setup) and was rated Mature in Australia and 18+ in Brazil.

If the app ever gets chat, comments, uploads or purchases, start a new questionnaire: questions 2, 3 and 6 change.
