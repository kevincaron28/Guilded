# Support Guilded

## Help keep Guilded free

Guilded is an independent project by [**KCaron**](https://kcaron.top/): a WoW addon, a Discord bot and a
companion that help guilds stay connected. The addon is free for noncommercial
use, and approved guilds can use the small hosted bot pilot for free.

Running the shared service takes resources. An optional coffee helps fund **bot hosting,
the PostgreSQL database, backups and ongoing maintenance**, so we can keep offering
Guilded at no cost to players and approved pilot guilds.

<div class="actions"><a class="button" href="https://ko-fi.com/wowguilded">Buy me a coffee · CA$3 →</a></div>

The official page is **[Guilded by KCaron on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/wowguilded)**.
The displayed coffee amount is **CA$3 (Canadian dollars)**. Support is entirely
voluntary. Review the amount, currency and whether a payment repeats on Ko-fi
before completing payment.

## What your support helps cover

- **Bot hosting:** keeping the shared Discord service available.
- **PostgreSQL and backups:** storing guild data and supporting recovery.
- **Maintenance:** fixing bugs, improving reliability and maintaining the addon
  and companion.
- **Future capacity:** helping the project support more guilds as resources allow.

## Always optional

No payment is required to download the addon, request bot access or ask for help.
Tips support the project; they are not a purchase of technical support or hosted
access. A contribution does not buy approval, priority, extra permissions or
guaranteed uptime. **KCaron must approve each Discord server before it can use
the hosted bot**, and places depend on available capacity.
[Request free pilot access](HOSTED_PILOT.md).

You can also help by reporting clear bugs and sharing feedback through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/kevincaron28/Guilded/issues). Thank you for
helping keep Guilded available to the community.
