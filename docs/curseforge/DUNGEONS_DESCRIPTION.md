# Guilded Dungeons 8.0.1

For **WoW Forever, Classic Era / Hardcore, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic and Retail**. Download the matching edition file. English, French, German and Spanish are included in the addon. The companion, website and bot remain English/French.

Guilded Dungeons records dungeon runs and available completion evidence, shows results and local scores, and can synchronize to public leaderboards. It is a small standalone addon for players without a guild or without the full Guilded addon. If both addons are installed, Guilded handles recording and Guilded Dungeons stays silent.

## 8.0.1 launch update

Complete shared French, German and Spanish translation catalogues (1,052 keys each), reviewed labels and formatting, with offline language layout checks. Includes the 8.0 security, privacy and versioned Retail overtime improvements. Preserve SavedVariables when upgrading.

[Edition downloads and release notes](https://github.com/kevincaron28/Guilded/releases/tag/v8.0.1) · [Choose your WoW edition](https://guilded.kcaron.top/editions/)

Classic and Retail include edition-specific routes and provisional benchmarks. Retail records supported native Mythic+ key, affix and timer evidence, with highest-key boards and a separate Raider.IO-inspired Guilded rating. Forever displays first and last names. Unsupported routes remain unrated. Benchmarks and Guilded ratings are provisional, not official Raider.IO or Blizzard scores. Forever gameplay data and calibration follow when the game is available; unperformed gameplay/two-PC checks remain documented follow-up.

## Install and connect

1. Download the file for your edition, close WoW and place the **GuildedDungeons** folder in your client’s `Interface/AddOns` directory. Preserve SavedVariables. Enable **Load out of date AddOns** if your client requests it.
2. Use `/gdungeons` or `/gdg` for status, results, check and score. Recording/local results do not require Discord or a companion.
3. For optional online synchronization, install the free [Microsoft Store companion](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N680XX3QFL9). It is Store-signed and updates through Microsoft Store; the separate GitHub EXE is unsigned. One companion supports all editions with separate profiles.
4. Use **Sign in with Discord** and type the companion’s confirmation code on the sign-in page. Select your server, or use the supported standalone-player connection from [your player page](https://guilded.kcaron.top/me/). Reload WoW before synchronization.

Finished eligible runs appear on [public leaderboards](https://guilded.kcaron.top/leaderboards/) by edition, dungeon, bracket, class, role and season. Online ranking depends on supported evidence and current season rules.

## Français

Choisissez le fichier de votre édition. L’addon inclut le français, l’anglais, l’allemand et l’espagnol ; le compagnon, le site et le bot restent en français et en anglais. Guilded Dungeons enregistre vos donjons et affiche les résultats et scores locaux. Avec Guilded installé, Guilded assure l’enregistrement et Guilded Dungeons reste silencieux.

Le [compagnon Microsoft Store gratuit](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N680XX3QFL9) est signé et reçoit ses mises à jour par le Store. Utilisez **Connexion avec Discord**, puis saisissez son code de confirmation sur la page de connexion. Les références et scores Guilded sont provisoires. Les données et l’étalonnage Forever attendent la disponibilité du jeu. Conservez vos SavedVariables lors des mises à jour.
