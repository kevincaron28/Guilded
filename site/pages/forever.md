# Get your guild ready for WoW Forever.

Guilded has a dedicated build for the Forever client: guild standings, EP/GP and loot, crafters,
and dungeon runs recorded in game, with free public leaderboards. English and French.

| When | What happens |

| **9 December 2026** | The first raids open. Blizzard's announced roadmap lists Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia's Lair (40). |

Dates come from Blizzard's published schedule; check the official announcements for changes.

## Launch week: for every player

1. **Install Guilded** (or the lighter **Guilded Dungeons**) from the [Forever download page](/editions/forever/).
   Use the folder beside the Forever game you launch.
2. **Play dungeons with your group.** Runs are recorded in game, even without a connection.
3. **Put your runs on the board.** Sign in with Discord in the [online companion](/companion/) and
   sync after playing. Your best routes count toward the [Season 1 leaderboards](/leaderboards/).
   Routes without verified targets stay unrated until they have them.

## Before raids open: for officers

1. **Add the bot and run `/setup start`** in your Discord ([officer guide](/officers/)), or stay
   addon-only if your guild prefers.
2. **Create your raid cores** for your 10- and 20-player teams with `/core setup`, and set GP prices
   with `/core items`.
3. **Pick your loot rules.** The [EP/GP guide](/docs/EPGP_GUIDE.html) explains point pools, minimum GP
   and bidding.
4. **Share the [member install guide](/docs/MEMBER_INSTALL.html)** so everyone has the addon before the first raid.
5. **Recruit.** Publish your guild page (`/guildpage create`, then `/guildpage publish`) and open
   recruitment with `/recruit set`: you appear in [Find a guild](/recruitment/guilds/).

## Why guilds pick Guilded for Forever

- **Works without Discord.** Standings, loot and recipes are shared in game between guild members.
- **Free.** No subscription, no account needed to use the addon.
- **Team scores.** Dungeon leaderboards rank whole-group clears, by level bracket, for characters and guilds.
- **Français inclus.** Every page, the addon and the bot speak English and French.
