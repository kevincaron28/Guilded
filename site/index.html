    Pair your own account

    Choose your game folders

    Supported browsers remember your saved-data and addon folders. Other browsers use manual files.

    Reload. Sync. Head back in.

BEFORE YOU HEAD IN

A few useful
answers.

Need a hand with setup?

Open the help guide ↗
Can I use Guilded without Discord?

Yes. Start with the WoW addon for local tools. Discord and the companion add optional synchronization. Availability depends on your game client and guild permissions.

Is the addon free?

Yes, Guilded is free for noncommercial use. Tips help fund hosting and maintenance, but no payment is required to download it, request pilot access or ask for help.

How does the hosted bot work?

Adding Guilded requests a place in a small free pilot. KCaron must approve your Discord server before setup and sync work; an invite does not guarantee a place. Follow the pilot guide or host independently.

Does every guildmate need a bot?

No. Your guild uses one bot. Discord-only members need no addon. Players who want WoW sync install the addon and pair their own companion.

Can I switch between browser and desktop?

Yes, with a fresh personal pairing code. Pairing a new companion replaces the previous link for your account. Finish any pending uploads before switching.

What about raid markers and boss plans?

Raid tools were removed from Guilded v6. A separate leader addon is future work and is not available here.
