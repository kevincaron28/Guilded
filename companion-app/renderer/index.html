          EXTRAS

          Tools

          Export your guild's data to a spreadsheet, or bring it in from another addon.

          Export to spreadsheet

          CSV files for Excel or Google Sheets, one per table, read from your Guilded.lua on this computer. Accents show correctly in Excel. In WoW, /reload first so the file holds your latest data.

          The installed companion asks for a folder and saves the files there.

          Import from Guild Roster Manager

          Convert a GRM SavedVariables backup into a local Guilded archive. No upload or game connection is needed. Source history stays separate from Guilded points and Discord ownership.

          Off by default: officer notes, custom notes and all logs are excluded because logs may contain private notes. The archive is readable data; keep private exports on a trusted device.

          Import loot history

          Bring RCLootCouncil or Gargul history into a local officer archive. Review character mappings here, then confirm in-game. No points or live awards change.

          Use RCLootCouncil CSV or JSON, or Gargul JSON or TMB CSV. JSON preserves more detail. Export a smaller date range if the archive is too large.

          Review character mappings

          Set each destination as Name-Realm. These exports do not verify guild membership or game edition.

            Sync filters
            
          
            Guild connections
            Keep a connection and saved-data folder for each guild or edition. On Windows, enable background sync for each connection you want to keep updated. Select a connection to view or edit it.
            Removed by mistake or picked the wrong server? Select it above, then Remove this server. It only forgets the connection on this PC and stops uploading there; nothing is deleted on the server.
            Enabled connections keep syncing when you view another connection. When connections share an addon folder, only the selected connection writes standings there.

            HERE WHEN YOU NEED IT
            Help & privacy
            Know what your companion does, and stay in control.
            Get and update the companion
            Guilded Companion · Microsoft Store
            The Windows companion watches selected game files and writes WoW standings back to your addon.
            Both versions update themselves. The Microsoft Store version is Store-signed and updated by the Store: to update right away, choose Open Microsoft Store on Home. The direct download from the website installs updates when you quit or restart it, or at once with Update now on Home; it is unsigned, so Windows may warn the first time you run its installer. The companion is optional for the addon alone, but needed to share data with Discord and the website. It runs on Windows only. The WoW addon is still required.
            Your data, explained
            Privacy policy
            WoW sends the addon's structured export. The bot checks your current Discord membership and restricts personal accounts to their own characters. Officers keep existing review rules.
            Your game files are read locally. Your Discord connection and your settings are saved on this PC, so restarting does not require pairing again. Use Remove data & quit in Connection & setup when you are finished with this PC.
            Export to spreadsheet
            In Tools, Export to spreadsheet turns your Guilded addon data into CSV files: standings (EP, GP, PR), loot history, attendance, raids and dungeon scores. The companion saves them in a folder you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere.
            Open them in Excel or Google Sheets (File > Import). Officers can get Discord's own ledger, loot, attendance and standings with /officer export. In game, /guilded sheet copies one table to paste.
            

            Something isn't syncing?
            • Not linked yet? Open Link everything: it shows what is missing and the one next step.
            • Use Test connection in setup.
            • Choose Sign in with Discord again if the link was revoked or replaced.
            • Leaderboards only: make sure step 2 points at your account's SavedVariables folder, where GuildedDungeons.lua (or Guilded.lua) is saved.
            • In WoW, /reload before syncing.
            • Review Activity or share a support summary with your officer.
