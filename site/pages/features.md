# Your guild tools, within reach.

Start with the WoW addon. Add the companion and Discord when you want to share data with your guild. Quebec Gold is a community using Guilded; Guilded is also available to other guilds.

## In game, without Discord

Use the addon for guild recipes, the guild map, attendance and loot workflows. Availability depends on your game client and guild permissions. You can use the addon without connecting a Discord account or installing the companion.

[Get Guilded on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/guilded) · [Installation guide](../docs/MEMBER_INSTALL.html)

## WoW standings in your browser

Open the companion, pair your account and select your saved addon data. After playing, reload WoW and choose **Sync now**. Bring the updated standings back into the addon, then reload again. Supported browsers can remember your folders; other browsers use manual upload and download.

[Open the companion](/companion/) · [Connection checklist](../docs/ONLINE_COMPANION_CHECK.html)

## Wishlists, cores and prices

In the companion, choose **Wishlist** to edit your own wanted items. Raid leaders can use **Cores & prices** to manage core rules, rosters and item prices. Your Discord permissions determine what you can view or change; pairing does not grant officer access.

These are existing companion screens. Open **Connection & setup** first if you have not paired this browser. Get your personal WoW code with `/character pair` in your guild's Discord and enter it only in the companion.

## Your Path of Exile 2 journal

The companion's **Path of Exile 2** page shows your personal map-area observations. The Windows companion supports automatic log watching; browser mode uses a selected log file. Enable sharing deliberately and choose your character, league and mode.

A visit does not prove a completed map, a kill, a drop or party participation. Observations award no automatic points. This is a personal journal, not a public guild roster.

## Organize together in Discord

Guilded's bot provides raid signups, community activities and polls. The hosted pilot needs owner approval before setup works. Other guilds may host their own bot.
