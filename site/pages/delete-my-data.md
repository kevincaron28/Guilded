<h1>Delete my data</h1>
<p>In your guild's Discord server, type <code>/delete-my-data</code>. Discord shows who you are, the bot tells you what will be removed, and nothing is deleted until you press the confirm button.</p>
<p>You left the server, or you only use the addon? Ask in the <a href="https://discord.gg/YjzFwtP64V">Guilded support community</a> for a private conversation. <a href="/docs/PRIVACY.html#choices-access-and-deletion">What is kept and for how long</a>.</p>
