## Dungeon runs and seasons

The dungeon finder's **Guilded** tab shows your recent runs, personal progress, guild rankings and past seasons. Runs are recorded without a connection; the [public leaderboards](/leaderboards/) need the companion. Public scoring rules are shared by every guild and frozen for the season. Unverified routes remain unrated.

Prefer just dungeons? **Guilded Dungeons** is the standalone tracker, with no guild
required. Sign in with Discord to sync finished runs, see your public player profile,
and manage your connection on [My Guilded](/me/).

[Dungeon setup and troubleshooting](../docs/DUNGEONS_GUIDE.html) · [Player profiles](/players/)

## Recruitment and open raids

Find a permanent guild or raid core, or fill an open spot for one raid night.
The [recruitment board](/recruitment/) lists vacancies from opt-in Discord raid
signups. Host officers keep control of the roster. Other guilds can show the feed
in an open-raids channel; full Guilded can browse a recent companion snapshot in game.

## The companion

A free Windows app, optional for the addon alone but needed for Discord and website features. Get it from [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N680XX3QFL9) (direct download from this website as a fallback). Connect your account, choose your saved addon data, then **Sync now** after playing. Export to spreadsheet works without connecting.

- **Wishlist:** your wanted items.
- **Cores & prices:** core rules, rosters and item prices, for raid leaders.
- **Path of Exile 2:** a personal journal of the map areas you visit. <!--poe-->

Your Discord permissions decide what you can see or change.

[Get the companion](/companion/)

## Discord

The bot adds raid signups, activities and polls. A new server starts on **Guilded Lite** (setup, help, bug reports, dungeon groups, basic signups); approval unlocks addon sync, EP/GP, seasons and the guild page. You can also host your own bot.
