# Google Play content rating (IARC questionnaire) — Guilded

Where to fill it in: Play Console → **Policy → App content → Content ratings → Start questionnaire**. Use the
support contact email from [play-listing.md](play-listing.md), not a personal address.

## Category

**All Other App Types** (utility, productivity, communication or other). Why not the other categories:

- Not a game: it is a companion for a game.
- Not "Social networking, forums, blogs and UGC sharing": members cannot post messages, chat, comment or upload media.
  What other members see is game data: characters, ranks, last-seen positions inside the game, wishlist item names.
- Not "Reference, news or educational": the News tab is one of five tabs and only shows headlines that link out.
