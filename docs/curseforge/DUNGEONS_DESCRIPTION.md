# Guilded Dungeons

For **WoW Forever**. Addon in English, French, German and Spanish; companion, website and bot in English and French.

Guilded Dungeons records your dungeon runs, shows results and local scores, and can sync to the public leaderboards. It is a small standalone addon for players without a guild or without the full Guilded addon. If both addons are installed, Guilded records and Guilded Dungeons stays silent.

## What it does

- Records each run: party, bosses, deaths and completion evidence, including the Forever final bosses.
- Stats by dungeon, boss splits, a traced death map and local scores.
- Monthly seasons with weekly and monthly boards, level brackets (15-29, 30-44, 45-59, 60), personal records and a record card when you set one.
- Optional online sync to the [public leaderboards](https://guilded.kcaron.top/leaderboards/) by dungeon, bracket, class, role and season, with character pictures and share cards.

Scores are Guilded's own, based on supported evidence and the current season rules. They are not official Blizzard scores. Forever characters show first and last names.

## Install and connect

1. Download the file, close WoW and put the **GuildedDungeons** folder in `_forever_/Interface/AddOns`. Keep your SavedVariables. Enable **Load out of date AddOns** if asked.
2. Use `/gdungeons` or `/gdg` for status, results and score. Recording and local results need no Discord and no companion.
3. For online sync, install the free [Microsoft Store companion](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N680XX3QFL9) (Store-signed, updates itself; the GitHub EXE is unsigned). It sets itself up and syncs every WoW account on the PC.
4. Choose **Sign in with Discord**, pick your server or the standalone-player connection from [your player page](https://guilded.kcaron.top/me/), then `/reload` before syncing.

## Français

Guilded Dungeons enregistre vos donjons pour **WoW Forever**, affiche les résultats, statistiques et scores locaux, et peut les envoyer aux classements publics. Avec Guilded installé, Guilded enregistre et Guilded Dungeons reste silencieux. Saisons mensuelles, records personnels, carte des morts. Installez le dossier **GuildedDungeons** dans `_forever_/Interface/AddOns` et conservez vos SavedVariables.

Le [compagnon Microsoft Store gratuit](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N680XX3QFL9) est facultatif : **Connexion avec Discord**, puis `/reload`. Les scores Guilded ne sont pas des scores officiels de Blizzard.
