<p class="eyebrow">GUIDE · LINKING</p>
<h1 id="link-title">Link your <em>character.</em></h1>
<p>Linking tells Guilded which WoW characters are yours, so your guild’s Discord and the website show them. With the companion it is one sign-in and one <code>/reload</code>: nothing to type or copy.</p>
<p class="small">Not installed yet? Start with <a href="/guide/install/">Install the addon</a>. You also need to be in your guild’s Discord server.</p>
</section>
<div class="start-paths">
<section class="start-path" aria-labelledby="companion-link-title">
<h2 id="companion-link-title">With the Windows companion (the easiest)</h2>
<ol class="start-list">
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><h3>Press Sign in with Discord</h3></div><p>Open the companion window and press <strong>Sign in with Discord</strong> (optional: it syncs without it).</p></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><h3>Choose your server and press Connect</h3></div><p>Your browser opens (it can hide behind the companion). Sign in to Discord if asked, choose your guild’s server, then press <strong>Connect</strong>. Go back to the companion. If the page asks for a code, type the one the companion shows.</p><p class="small">Discord may email you to confirm a new sign-in location: open it, choose <strong>Yes, it’s me</strong>, then carry on.</p></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><h3>Type /reload in game</h3></div><p>In WoW, log in with your character and type <code>/reload</code> once. WoW saves your data and the companion reads it. If the companion has not noticed yet, press <strong>Sync now</strong> and type <code>/reload</code> again.</p></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><h3>See that it is linked</h3></div><p>You are linked when every line in the companion is green and the <strong>Synced as</strong> line shows your Discord name. Your characters are on your My Guilded page. Guilded also sends you a short Discord message naming the linked characters. Your main and every alt that logged in on this PC are linked together.</p><p>In Discord, <code>/character list</code> shows your main and alts at any time.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
<section class="start-path" id="no-companion" aria-labelledby="name-title">
<h2 id="name-title">Without the companion: send the character’s name</h2>
<p>No Windows PC, or you would rather not install the companion? Do this. Nothing to copy or type in game.</p>
<ol class="start-list">
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><h3>Send the full name to the bot</h3></div><p>Send the Guilded bot a Discord direct message with your character’s full name (First Last). You can also type it in your server’s guild-invite channel.</p></li>
<li class="start-step"><div class="start-head"><span class="start-num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><h3>Wait for the next sync</h3></div><p>The link is confirmed at the next sync that shows the character in the guild, from an officer or a guildmate’s companion. Guilded then sends you a Discord message saying which character is linked.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
<section class="start-path" aria-labelledby="second-title">
<h2 id="second-title">Link a second character</h2>
<p>Most of the time there is nothing to do.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Same PC, with the companion:</strong> log in with the alt, type <code>/reload</code>, then press <strong>Sync now</strong> in the companion. It joins the others.</li>
<li><strong>Another PC:</strong> install the companion there too and press <strong>Sign in with Discord</strong> again. Your other PC stays linked.</li>
<li><strong>A character is missing?</strong> Send the bot its full name (First Last) in a direct message, or type it in the guild-invite channel. Check with <code>/character list</code>.</li>
<li><strong>A character should not be there?</strong> In Discord, type <code>/character unlink</code> and choose it.</li>
</ul>
<h3>It did not work?</h3>
<div class="start-help"><details><summary>The browser did not open, or I lost the page</summary><p>The window may be behind the companion or in the taskbar. If it is gone, press <strong>Cancel</strong> in the companion, then <strong>Sign in with Discord</strong> again.</p></details><details><summary>No character shows after /reload</summary><p>Check that Guilded is ticked in the game’s addon list, type <code>/reload</code>, then press <strong>Sync now</strong> and wait a minute. Still empty: send the bot your character’s full name in a direct message.</p></details><details><summary>It says I am not in a server that uses Guilded</summary><p>Join your guild’s Discord (ask an officer for the invite), then sign in again from the companion.</p></details><details><summary>I want to undo it</summary><p>In the companion, <strong>Connected devices</strong> removes this PC on the website, and <strong>Advanced → Reset this companion</strong> clears it. In Discord, <code>/character unlink</code> removes a character.</p></details></div>
